Transcript-Equivalent Pragmatic Contrast in Speech Representations

Deployed speech-to-speech systems never hear audio. They hear discrete tokens. Do those tokens still carry what a speaker meant?

Systems such as Moshi convert speech into discrete tokens before any language model sees it, so whatever the tokeniser discards is simply unavailable downstream. If it discards pragmatic force, a system can recover every word correctly and still misread how the speaker meant them. Short phrases make this sharp, because yeah, sure and great each perform opposite speech acts, sincere agreement or sarcastic dismissal, while the transcript stays identical. Earlier probing work showed that speech representations recover pragmatic phenomena better than text, but the utterances in those corpora also differed in wording, so delivery remained confounded with word choice. This study removes that confound by holding the word constant and probing frozen representations, meaning the pretrained models are never updated and only a small linear classifier is trained, so any accuracy reflects what the representation already encodes. Four families are compared, self-supervised encoders (WavLM, HuBERT), a supervised ASR encoder (Whisper), the tokeniser deployed systems actually consume (Mimi), and transcript-only baselines.

Lexical control, the within-word test averaged over the eight phrases, gives WavLM 0.659 against text-plus-discourse 0.534, so the contrast survives even when the word itself cannot help. Chance here is about 0.33, the mean of the permutation null, not the 0.196 a majority-class predictor would score. Against that line the three continuous encoders sit far above, while Mimi at 0.352 clears it only narrowly, though still significantly (p ≤ 0.01). Pooled target-only text sits above chance only because the eight phrases differ in stance base rate, which is why the within-word figure is the clean test.
Where it lives. WavLM peaks at layer 20 of 24 and falls away above it, consistent with upper layers shedding paralinguistics, while Whisper plateaus across its top third. Inside Mimi the loss is not localised, since refinement codebooks 1 to 7 score 0.33 to 0.37 individually and stacking them adds nothing.
