Topic.  Speech-to-speech systems never receive audio, they receive discrete tokens, so anything the tokeniser discards is unavailable to everything downstream.

Question.  When the same word carries opposite pragmatic force, a sincere yeah against a sarcastic one, is that contrast still present in the representations deployed systems consume, and at which stage of the pipeline is it lost?

Status / Approach / findings
  • 873 hand-labelled podcast clips across eight phrases, with the word held constant so only delivery varies. Linear probes on frozen representations.
  • Continuous encoders keep the contrast. Quantisation is not where it is lost. The codec encoder costs about three times as much, on two different codecs.
  • The surprise is that distillation helps. A purely acoustic codec keeps roughly half of what the WavLM-distilled one does.
