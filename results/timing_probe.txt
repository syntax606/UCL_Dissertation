DOES STANCE SURVIVE A READOUT THAT RESPECTS TIME?
Representation held fixed, readout varied. 'shuffled' applies the same
readout to frame-shuffled clips, so it matches on dimensionality and on
every feature's marginal distribution but destroys order.

Every score is the mean over 25 independent episode-to-fold
partitions, with its sd. A single partition is not a stable measurement:
holding everything else constant, fold assignment alone moves macro-F1 by
sd 0.010 and range up to 0.06, and sklearn changed its own assignment
between 1.7 and 1.9. Partitions are defined here, not by GroupKFold, so
these numbers do not depend on which sklearn is installed.

'order' is PAIRED: real minus shuffled on each partition separately, then
averaged. Partition noise is shared by both and cancels, so its sd is much
smaller than the sd of either column. t is order / stderr(order).

Read: 'order' is the readout's gain over its own shuffled control, so it
is the part attributable to timing rather than to having more dimensions.
If it is near zero everywhere, stance is not carried temporally and the
pooled figures were not understating anything. If it is large for the
codecs and small for the continuous encoders, the loss reported in [Ch.4]
is partly an artefact of the readout rather than a property of the codec.

A single partition would not settle this. Fold assignment alone moves the
score by about as much as the effect being looked for, so an unpaired 0.02
would be indistinguishable from a coin toss. The paired difference is what
carries the claim, and its sd is the scale against which to read it.
