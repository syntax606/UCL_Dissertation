[5.4] argues that level cues are directly reconstructive and carry arousal,
while contour, quality and rate carry stance and cost a codec little to shed.
Rankings WITHIN a task are the comparable quantity. Margins across the two
tasks are not, since stance is three-way and arousal binary, so the two tables
must not be divided into each other.
