Read: 'order' is the readout's gain over its own shuffled control, so it
is the part attributable to timing rather than to having more dimensions.
If it is near zero everywhere, stance is not carried temporally and the
pooled figures were not understating anything. If it is large for the
codecs and small for the continuous encoders, the loss reported in [Ch.4]
is partly an artefact of the readout rather than a property of the codec.

A single partition would not settle this. Fold assignment alone moves the
