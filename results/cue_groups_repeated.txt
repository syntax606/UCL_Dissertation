WHAT THIS SETTLES
  Most stance per feature: contour (F0/loudness dynamics), +0.083 from 12 features.
  Contour carries +0.083 from 12 features. Coarse rate carries +0.024 from 6.
  Level, how loud and how high the phrase sits, carries +0.060 from 9.
  So the two senses of temporal come apart. How pitch and loudness move carries
  stance; how fast the phrase runs does not. [4.6] and [5.2] are about different
  quantities and only look contradictory if the distinction is left implicit.

  Against arousal the ordering differs, which is what makes the two axes separable:
    voice quality (jitter/HNR)        +0.107
    spectral and formant              +0.088
    level (loudness, F0 mean)         +0.085
