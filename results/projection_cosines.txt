PROJECTION-SPACE DIAGNOSTICS for the quantisation ladder
The residual quantiser works in a projected space. The encoder latent and the
quantiser's reconstructed output are therefore NOT commensurate. The ladder
compares input_proj(latent) against the summed codebook vectors, before the
output projection. These numbers are what licenses that choice.
